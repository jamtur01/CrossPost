# CrossPost

A native macOS app for reading and posting to Mastodon and Bluesky side by side.

## Build & test

The Xcode project is generated by XcodeGen from `project.yml` (it is **not**
checked in — `CrossPost.xcodeproj/` is gitignored). Regenerate after changing
`project.yml`, dependencies, or adding/removing source files:

```sh
xcodegen generate
```

- Build: `./build.sh` (add `--run` to launch, `--release` for Release).
- Build directly: `xcodebuild build -project CrossPost.xcodeproj -scheme CrossPost -destination 'platform=macOS'`
- Test: `xcodebuild test -project CrossPost.xcodeproj -scheme CrossPost -destination 'platform=macOS'`

## Layout

- `Sources/Core` — platform-agnostic models, validation, posting, and the
  `FeedService` adapters over TootSDK (Mastodon) and ATProtoKit (Bluesky).
  Core never imports SwiftUI.
- `Sources/App` — SwiftUI views and `@Observable` models (`FeedPanelModel`,
  `ComposeModel`, `PostList`).
- `Tests/` — `CoreTests` and `AppTests`, both in the `CrossPostTests` target.

## Known gaps (parsed but not yet surfaced)

These `FeedPost` fields are populated by the Mastodon adapter (and honored on
outgoing posts) but are **not shown when reading a feed** — intentional TODOs,
not bugs:

- **`spoilerText` (content warnings):** a CW'd Mastodon post currently shows its
  body directly. Needs a collapsible content-warning row (tap to reveal).
- **`isSensitive` (sensitive media):** sensitive images render unblurred. Needs
  a blur-until-tapped gate.

`visibility` is surfaced (a glyph by the timestamp for non-public posts).
